Tamsin Hollow Roasters: Hollow Reserve

Approved by Mahesh Gadi on 2026-11-18 23:34 UTC.

Strategy

Positioning: For home filter brewers who already buy specialty whole-bean coffee and care where it comes from, Hollow Reserve is a washed Caturra from Finca Monte Tamsal in Huila, Colombia, that tastes of red apple, panela, and cocoa. We have bought from Finca Monte Tamsal for 7 consecutive harvests.

Concept: Know your cup: a plainspoken, barista-across-the-counter introduction to one farm, one process, and three tasting notes. Each asset names Finca Monte Tamsal, explains what washed means in the brand's own words, and tells you what to taste, so a new brewer and a longtime subscriber can both see exactly where the 2026 harvest comes from.

  • Hollow Reserve is a washed Caturra from Finca Monte Tamsal in Huila, Colombia, roasted from beans harvested in 2026. (F-002, F-011)
  • It tastes of red apple, panela, and cocoa. (F-003)
  • Washed coffee: the fruit is removed from the bean before it dries, which gives a clean, bright cup. (F-002)
  • We have bought from Finca Monte Tamsal for 7 consecutive harvests. (F-004)
  • Roasted within 48 hours of shipping. (F-001)
  • Best Washed Coffee, 2026 Quenby Valley Cup Awards (F-008)
  • $19 per 12 oz bag. Free shipping on orders over $40 in the contiguous US. (F-005, F-006)
  • Market context: in the 2026 National Coffee Data Trends report, 47% of U.S. adults had specialty coffee in the past day, and 35% of past-day specialty coffee drinkers said flavor is part of what makes a cup of coffee 'specialty coffee'. That makes a clear, named tasting profile a good lead for this audience. (E-22, E-23)

Channel plans

Internal planning, not for publication.

  • Search ads: Catch home brewers already looking for specialty whole-bean coffee and bring them to the landing page with the farm, the process, and the tasting notes. We are targeting searchers who may be ready to buy, so the washed Caturra from Finca Monte Tamsal, the red apple, panela, and cocoa notes (F-002, F-003), and the $19 per 12 oz bag price (F-005) give new buyers concrete reasons to click. Flavor matters to specialty drinkers (E-22).
  • Instagram: Show the farm name, the three tasting notes, and the award in plain, curious language, and explain washed coffee with the glossary wording. A visual feed suits a flavor-led, origin-led story. It reaches new brewers with F-002, F-003, and F-008. We have bought from Finca Monte Tamsal for 7 consecutive harvests.
  • Email: Introduce Hollow Reserve to existing subscribers, leading with “We have bought from Finca Monte Tamsal for 7 consecutive harvests.” and the subscriber quote. Include “Hollow Reserve is roasted from beans harvested in 2026.” For existing subscribers, lead with: “We have bought from Finca Monte Tamsal for 7 consecutive harvests.” (F-004) Also highlight “Hollow Reserve is roasted from beans harvested in 2026.” (F-011) Include the customer voice: "I set my alarm ten minutes early for this one." - Dana R., subscriber since 2024 (F-009). Roasted within 48 hours of shipping (F-001) is a useful detail for them.
  • Landing page: Turn first-time visitors into first orders by laying out the farm, the process, the tasting notes, the roast timing, the price, and the shipping offer in one place.
